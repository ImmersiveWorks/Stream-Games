# Stream Games
![Version Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Alpha_Release-0.0.1-orange?style=for-the-badge)

**Interactive mini-games for your livestreams.**  
Designed for streamers, by streamers. Stream Games brings a new layer of interactivity to your livestreams, allowing your viewers to compete in mini-games displayed directly on your stream.

## ⚠️ Early Access

**Stream Games** is currently in early alpha testing. The software is free to download and use, but please be aware that you may encounter bugs, unfinished features, or unexpected behavior.

If you encounter an issue, please report it through the [Issues](https://github.com/ImmersiveWorks/Stream-Games/issues) page to help us improve the software. All visuals and features are subject to change during development.

> **A note about paid subscriptions**
>
> **Stream Games** includes optional paid subscriptions that unlock additional configuration options. During this early testing phase, we recommend that you do **not** purchase a subscription, unless you have been invited to do so by a member of the development team.

## ⚙️ Getting Started

1. Download and run the latest Stream Games installer from the [Releases](https://github.com/ImmersiveWorks/Stream-Games/releases) page.
2. Launch the newly installed Stream Games launcher.
3. Connect your Twitch account.
4. Configure your preferred game settings.
5. Get ready to play!

## 🎮 Available Games

**Stream Games** currently features two interactive mini-games.

### Skydive

![Skydive](https://github.com/ImmersiveWorks/Stream-Games/blob/main/assets/skydive.png)

It's all about timing! Players attempt to land their avatars on a moving platform. Can you stick the landing?

### Battle Royale

![Battle Royale](https://github.com/ImmersiveWorks/Stream-Games/blob/main/assets/battle_royale.png)

Enter the arena and fight to survive! Players compete in a shrinking arena, battling to become the last one standing.
